Welcome, Weary Traveler

Have you lost something strange? Found something stranger? You've come to the right place.

This is the official website of our Lost And Found office, where the world's most puzzling lost items wait for their owners. Every item here was found somewhere it shouldn't have been, and none have been claimed yet.

How It Works

  1. Browse the Found Items page to see what we've collected.
  2. Spot something that belongs to you (or that you're brave enough to claim).
  3. File a claim and wait for our very slow response.
